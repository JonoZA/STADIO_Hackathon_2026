Our Brief

The problem we have set out to solve, and how team hAIre team answers it.

WHOSE problem are we solving?

Recruiters that are struggling to find ideal candidates due to not having enough time to filter the vast volume of candidates applying to a specific job.

WHAT is the problem?

Recruiters are not able to effectively process hundreds or thousands of résumés or lack the software to due it effectively.

WHY is this a problem?

Many recruiters are overwhelmed with the number of applicants that apply for a job, thus recruiters are unable to accurately pick the best candidate for the role.

The solution

hAIre

hAIre — the AI powered hiring tool — gives recruiters the power to rapidly process thousands of CVs, with the surety that the best candidate makes it to the top of the pile.
