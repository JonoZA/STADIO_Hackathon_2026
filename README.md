# hAIre

### STADIO SOIT Hackathon 2026 — Team hAIre

hAIre is an AI-powered recruitment platform that automatically analyses CVs, extracts candidate information, and ranks applicants based on how well they match a specific job's requirements.

The goal is to reduce the time recruiters spend manually screening CVs while providing a consistent and explainable first-stage screening process.

## How It Works

```text
Candidate Application
        ↓
     CV Upload
        ↓
   PDF Extraction
        ↓
   Google Gemini
        ↓
Structured Candidate Profile
        ↓
Job Requirements
        ↓
 Matching Algorithm
        ↓
  Match Score / 100
